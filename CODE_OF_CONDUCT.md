# Code of conduct

This project adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be technical and kind. Critique the byte layout, not the person.
- Assume the other person has hardware you do not.
- Publish evidence with claims. "It works on mine" needs the model and firmware.
- Respect privacy: never post another person's captures without stripping addresses.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, sexualised language, doxxing, publishing private captures, and
sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer through a private security advisory or the email
in the GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed and answered. Consequences range from a
private warning to a permanent ban, at the maintainer's discretion.

The full text is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
